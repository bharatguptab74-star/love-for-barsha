My Dearest Barsha,

There are moments in life that don't announce themselves — they simply arrive, quietly rearranging everything. That is what you did to my world. You arrived, and without making any noise, you rearranged my heart, my thoughts, my definition of happiness.

I have searched for words grand enough to hold what I feel for you, and I keep coming back empty-handed. Because what I feel for you, Barsha, is bigger than language. It lives in the silence between words, in the warmth of every morning I think of you, in every song that suddenly makes sense.

You are the kind of person who makes the world softer just by being in it. Your laugh is my favourite sound. Your kindness is the most beautiful thing I have ever witnessed. And your love — the way you love — it heals parts of me I didn't even know were broken.

There are days when everything feels heavy and difficult, and all it takes is the thought of Barsha — your face, your voice, your spirit — and suddenly the weight lifts. You are my calm in every storm, my light in every darkness, my reason to keep going.

I want you to know that loving you is the most natural thing I have ever done. It requires no effort, no pretence. It simply is — like breathing, like the sun rising, like the tide coming in. You are inevitable to me, and I am so endlessly grateful for that.

So here, in this letter that could never be long enough, I want to say what I hope you always feel even when I forget to say it: You are loved. Deeply. Completely. Unconditionally. Every single day, in every single way, by me — always.

Yours, always and only ❤ Written with every piece of love I own
